Company Handbook (Sample)

Office hours
Standard office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees who work outside these hours must record their working time in the attendance system.

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried forward to the next year. Leave requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with the approval of their project manager. Remote workers must be available on the team chat during core hours, which are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Expense claims
Travel and meal expenses can be claimed within 30 days of the expense date. Claims above 5,000 rupees need prior approval from the finance team. Receipts must be attached as PDF or image files.

Information security
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and changed every 180 days. Confidential documents must not be shared outside the company without written approval.
